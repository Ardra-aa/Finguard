**CSR Policy 2019**
**(SUPERSEDED — pre Companies Amendment Act 2020)**

**1. Purpose**
BharatFinServ Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "the Company") is committed to operating its business in a socially responsible manner, recognizing its role as a responsible corporate citizen within the Indian and global communities it serves. This Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Policy outlines the framework for the Company’s CSR activities, establishing our commitment to sustainable development, ethical practices, and the welfare of society. This policy is formulated in compliance with the provisions of Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, and the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules, 2014, as amended from time to time, existing prior to the Companies (Amendment) Act, 2020. The Company endeavors to contribute to societal well-being through initiatives that create long-term value for stakeholders and the communities in which we operate.

**2. Scope**
This policy applies to all CSR activities undertaken by BharatFinServ Ltd., including its wholly-owned subsidiaries and joint ventures as applicable, within India. It covers all projects, programs, and initiatives that fall within the purview of Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013, as it stood in 2019. The policy shall guide the Company's decision-making process for identifying, implementing, monitoring, and evaluating CSR projects, ensuring alignment with our core values and regulatory requirements.

**3. Definitions**
For the purpose of this Policy:
*   **"Act"** means the Companies Act, 2013.
*   **"Board"** means the Board of Directors of BharatFinServ Ltd.
*   **"Company"** refers to BharatFinServ Ltd.
*   **"CSR Committee"** means the Corporate Social Responsibility Committee of the Board constituted under Section 135 of the Act.
*   **"CSR Policy"** means this Corporate Social Responsibility Policy of the Company, as amended from time to time.
*   **"CSR Activities"** means the activities undertaken by the Company in India as specified in Schedule VII of the Act and the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules, 2014, excluding activities undertaken in the normal course of business.
*   **"Net Profit"** means the net profit of the Company as per its financial statement prepared in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Act, but shall not include profits arising from overseas branches or dividends received from other companies covered under Section 135.

**4. Policy Details**

**4.1. CSR Committee**
The Board of Directors shall constitute a CSR Committee comprising at least three (3) Directors, one of whom shall be an Independent Director.
The CSR Committee shall be responsible for:
    a. Formulating and recommending to the Board, a comprehensive CSR Policy indicating the activities to be undertaken by the Company as specified in Schedule VII.
    b. Recommending the amount of expenditure to be incurred on the CSR activities.
    c. Monitoring the CSR Policy from time to time.
    d. Instituting a transparent monitoring mechanism for the implementation of the CSR projects or programs or activities undertaken by the Company.
    e. Ensuring that the administrative overheads associated with CSR activities do not exceed 5% of the total CSR expenditure for the financial year.
The CSR Committee shall meet at least twice a year.

**4.2. Focus Areas for CSR Activities**
In alignment with Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013 (pre-2020 amendment), BharatFinServ Ltd. shall focus its CSR activities primarily on:
    a. Eradicating hunger, poverty, and malnutrition, promoting health care including preventive health care and sanitation including contribution to the Swachh Bharat Kosh set-up by the Central Government for the promotion of sanitation and making available safe drinking water.
    b. Promoting education, including special education and employment enhancing vocational skills, especially among children, women, elderly, and the differently-abled, and livelihood enhancement projects.
    c. Promoting gender equality, empowering women, setting up homes and hostels for women and orphans; setting up old age homes, day care centers, and such other facilities for senior citizens and measures for reducing inequalities faced by socially and economically backward groups.
    d. Ensuring environmental sustainability, ecological balance, protection of flora and fauna, animal welfare, agroforestry, conservation of natural resources, and maintaining the quality of soil, air, and water including contribution to the Clean Ganga Fund set-up by the Central Government for rejuvenation of river Ganga.
    e. Protection of national heritage, art, and culture including restoration of buildings and sites of historical importance and works of art; setting up public libraries; promotion and development of traditional arts and handicrafts.
    f. Measures for the benefit of armed forces veterans, war widows, and their dependents.
    g. Training to promote rural sports, nationally recognized sports, paralympic sports, and Olympic sports.
    h. Contribution to the Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund or any other fund set up by the Central Government for socio-economic development and relief and welfare of the Scheduled Castes, the Scheduled Tribes, other backward classes, minorities, and women.
    i. Contributions or funds provided to technology incubators located within academic institutions which are approved by the Central Government.
    j. Rural development projects.
    k. Slum area development.

**4.3. CSR Expenditure**
The Company shall strive to spend at least two percent (2%) of the average net profits of the Company made during the three immediately preceding financial years on CSR activities.
*   The "average net profit" shall be calculated in accordance with Section 198 of the Act.
*   Any surplus arising out of the CSR activities shall not form part of the business profit of the Company.
*   If the Company fails to spend the stipulated amount in any financial year, the Board shall, in its Report made under clause (o) of sub-section (3) of Section 134, specify the reasons for not spending the amount. There is no specific requirement under the current regulatory framework for transferring the unspent amount to a separate government fund.

**4.4. Implementation Modalities**
The Company may undertake its CSR activities through:
    a. A registered trust or a registered society or a company established under Section 8 of the Act, either established by the Company itself or along with any other company, or
    b. Any other registered trust or registered society or company established under Section 8 of the Act, not established by the Company, provided that such entity has an established track record of three years in undertaking similar programs or projects.
The Company may also collaborate with other companies for undertaking CSR projects or programs, provided that the CSR Committees of the respective companies are in a position to report separately on such projects or programs in accordance with the rules.

**4.5. Monitoring and Reporting**
The CSR Committee shall regularly monitor the progress of all CSR projects and programs. The Board shall ensure that the CSR activities undertaken are within the specified scope. An annual report on CSR activities, including details of projects, expenditure, and reasons for unspent amounts (if any), shall be prepared by the CSR Committee and approved by the Board for inclusion in the Board's Report in the Company's Annual Report.

**5. Responsibilities**
*   **Board of Directors:** Approve the CSR Policy, ensure compliance with Section 135, and ensure disclosure in the Board's Report.
*   **CSR Committee:** Formulate, recommend, and monitor the CSR Policy and projects, and recommend expenditure.
*   **Compliance Officer:** Ensure all regulatory filings and disclosures related to CSR are made accurately and timely.
*   **Designated Management Team (e.g., HR/Admin):** Implement the CSR projects approved by the Board, manage operational aspects, and provide regular reports to the CSR Committee.

**6. Review Cycle**
This Policy shall be reviewed by the CSR Committee annually, or as and when required due to changes in regulatory requirements or business strategy, and recommended to the Board for approval.

**7. Effective Date**
This Policy is effective from **April 1, 2019**.
