**BHARATFINSERV LTD.**
**CODE OF CONDUCT AND ETHICS POLICY**

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Access Level:** All Employees

---

**1. PURPOSE**

The BharatFinServ Ltd. Code of Conduct and Ethics Policy ("the Code") serves as a foundational guide for all individuals associated with our Company. Its primary purpose is to articulate the ethical principles, values, and standards of professional conduct expected from every employee, director, and officer. This Code underscores BharatFinServ's unwavering commitment to integrity, honesty, fairness, and accountability in all aspects of its operations, across its NBFC, insurance, asset management, and IT services businesses, and its global presence spanning Mumbai, Bengaluru, GIFT City, and Frankfurt. By fostering a culture of ethical behavior and regulatory compliance, this Code aims to protect the Company's reputation, safeguard stakeholder trust, and ensure sustainable growth.

**2. SCOPE**

This Code applies to all individuals employed by BharatFinServ Ltd. and its subsidiaries, affiliates, and joint ventures globally, regardless of their role, tenure, or location. This includes, but is not limited to, permanent employees, contractual employees, temporary staff, trainees, consultants, Key Managerial Personnel (KMPs), and all members of the Board of Directors (Executive, Non-Executive, and Independent Directors). The principles outlined herein govern all professional activities, interactions, and decisions made on behalf of or within the Company's business environment.

**3. DEFINITIONS**

*   **Company:** Refers to BharatFinServ Ltd. and all its domestic and international subsidiaries, affiliates, and joint ventures.
*   **Employee:** Any individual employed by the Company, including permanent, temporary, contractual staff, consultants, and trainees.
*   **Director:** A member of the Board of Directors of BharatFinServ Ltd.
*   **Key Managerial Personnel (KMP):** As defined under the Companies Act, 2013, including the CEO, CFO, Company Secretary, and Whole-Time Directors.
*   **Stakeholders:** Encompasses all parties with an interest in the Company, including employees, customers, investors, regulators, business partners, and the communities in which we operate.
*   **Confidential Information:** Any non-public information relating to the Company, its clients, customers, or business partners, including financial data, trade secrets, intellectual property, strategic plans, and personal data.
*   **Conflict of Interest:** A situation where an individual's personal interests (financial, familial, or otherwise) could potentially or actually influence their objective decision-making or actions in the Company's best interest.

**4. POLICY DETAILS**

**4.1 Ethical Conduct and Integrity**
BharatFinServ is committed to conducting its business with the highest standards of integrity, honesty, and fairness. All employees, directors, and officers are expected to act with utmost integrity, making decisions that are transparent, ethical, and in the best interest of the Company and its stakeholders. This includes avoiding situations that could create a conflict of interest, ensuring that personal interests do not supersede the Company's objectives. Bribery, corruption, illegal payments, fraud, and any form of unethical financial manipulation are strictly prohibited. We uphold principles of respect, dignity, and professional courtesy in all interactions, fostering a work environment free from harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.

**4.2 Compliance with Laws and Regulations**
Adherence to all applicable laws, rules, and regulations is paramount. All employees, directors, and KMPs must understand and comply with the legal framework governing our operations. This includes, but is not limited to, the Companies Act, 2013, SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines for NBFCs, IRDAI regulations for insurance, PMLA (Prevention of Money Laundering Act), IT Act, and relevant international laws such as GDPR for our EU subsidiary. Specifically, in compliance with **Section 149 of the Companies Act, 2013**, which outlines duties of directors and mandates a Code for Independent Directors, and **Schedule IV of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015**, mandating a Code of Conduct for Board of Directors and Senior Management, BharatFinServ extends these principles to all employees to ensure a holistic ethical framework.

**4.3 Protection and Proper Use of Company Assets & Information**
Employees are entrusted with the responsibility to protect and use Company assets, including physical property, intellectual property, and financial resources, efficiently and solely for legitimate business purposes. Misappropriation, theft, or unauthorized use of Company assets is strictly prohibited. Furthermore, safeguarding confidential information, trade secrets, and proprietary data is critical. Employees must not disclose or use Confidential Information for personal gain or to the detriment of the Company or its clients. This includes maintaining the privacy of customer data and adhering to data protection laws in all jurisdictions, including India and the EU.

**4.4 Fair Dealing and Anti-Discrimination**
BharatFinServ is committed to fair dealing with its employees, customers, suppliers, competitors, and other stakeholders. We believe in providing equal opportunities and fostering an inclusive workplace where diversity is valued. Discrimination or harassment based on race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic is strictly prohibited. All employees are expected to treat colleagues and external parties with respect and fairness, avoiding any form of exploitation, manipulation, concealment, abuse of privileged information, misrepresentation of material facts, or any other unfair-dealing practice.

**4.5 Reporting Violations and Non-Retaliation**
Every employee has a responsibility to report any suspected or actual violations of this Code, Company policies, or applicable laws. BharatFinServ encourages open communication and provides confidential channels for reporting concerns, including through the designated Ethics Committee, HR Department, or whistleblower mechanism, without fear of retaliation. The Company is committed to thoroughly investigating all reports made in good faith and taking appropriate corrective actions. Retaliation against any employee who reports a concern or cooperates in an investigation in good faith is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.

**5. RESPONSIBILITIES**

*   **Board of Directors:** Responsible for the ultimate oversight, approval, and enforcement of this Code, ensuring its effectiveness and alignment with the Company's values and legal obligations.
*   **Senior Management and KMPs:** Expected to lead by example, promote an ethical culture, ensure their teams understand and comply with the Code, and address ethical dilemmas promptly.
*   **Managers and Supervisors:** Accountable for implementing the Code within their respective teams, providing guidance, addressing concerns, and ensuring that team members receive adequate training and support.
*   **All Employees:** Expected to read, understand, and comply with all provisions of this Code, act ethically in all business dealings, report potential violations, and seek clarification when in doubt.
*   **Compliance Department/Legal Team:** Responsible for providing guidance on the Code, conducting training, monitoring compliance, investigating reported violations, and recommending updates to the Code.
*   **Ethics Committee:** Oversees the implementation of the Code, reviews significant ethical concerns, and recommends appropriate actions.

**6. REVIEW CYCLE**

This Code of Conduct and Ethics Policy will be reviewed annually by the Compliance Department and Legal Team, in consultation with the Ethics Committee. Any necessary amendments or updates, driven by changes in laws, regulations, business practices, or organizational structure, will be presented to the Board of Directors for approval.

**7. EFFECTIVE DATE**

This Code of Conduct and Ethics Policy is effective from **January 1, 2024**.
