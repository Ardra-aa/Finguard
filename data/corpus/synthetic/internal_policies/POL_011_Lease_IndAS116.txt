INTERNAL - BHARATFINSERV LTD. - FICTIONAL

**Lease Accounting Policy under Ind AS 116**

**1. Purpose**
This policy outlines BharatFinServ Ltd.'s (hereinafter "the Company") approach to accounting for leases in accordance with Indian Accounting Standard (Ind AS) 116, Leases. The objective is to ensure consistent and accurate recognition, measurement, presentation, and disclosure of lease transactions in the Company's financial statements, reflecting the economic substance of these arrangements and maintaining compliance with regulatory requirements and corporate governance standards.

**2. Scope**
This policy applies to all lease contracts entered into by BharatFinServ Ltd. and its subsidiaries (including those operating in Bengaluru, GIFT City IFSC, and Frankfurt EU) where the Company acts as a lessee or lessor. It covers all types of assets leased, including property, plant, and equipment, vehicles, and IT infrastructure. This policy is mandatory for all personnel involved in initiating, reviewing, approving, or recording lease transactions, and for those responsible for financial reporting.

**3. Definitions**
*   **Lease:** A contract, or part of a contract, that conveys the right to use an identified asset (the underlying asset) for a period of time in exchange for consideration.
*   **Lessee:** An entity that obtains the right to use an underlying asset for a period of time in exchange for consideration.
*   **Lessor:** An entity that provides the right to use an underlying asset for a period of time in exchange for consideration.
*   **Right-of-Use (ROU) Asset:** An asset that represents a lessee’s right to use an underlying asset for the lease term.
*   **Lease Liability:** A lessee’s obligation to make lease payments arising from a lease.
*   **Lease Term:** The non-cancellable period for which a lessee has the right to use an underlying asset, together with periods covered by an option to extend the lease if the lessee is reasonably certain to exercise that option, and periods covered by an option to terminate the lease if the lessee is reasonably certain not to exercise that option.
*   **Incremental Borrowing Rate:** The rate of interest that a lessee would have to pay to borrow funds over a similar term, and with a similar security, to obtain an asset of a similar value to the Right-of-Use asset in a similar economic environment.

**4. Policy Details**

**4.1. Lessee Accounting: Recognition and Initial Measurement**
Upon the commencement date of a lease, BharatFinServ Ltd., as a lessee, shall recognise a Right-of-Use (ROU) asset and a corresponding Lease Liability.
*   **ROU Asset:** Measured at cost, comprising the initial measurement of the Lease Liability, any lease payments made at or before the commencement date (less any lease incentives received), any initial direct costs incurred by the lessee, and an estimate of costs to dismantle and remove the underlying asset or restore the site, as required by the lease terms.
*   **Lease Liability:** Measured at the present value of the lease payments that are unpaid at the commencement date. The lease payments are discounted using the interest rate implicit in the lease, if that rate can be readily determined. If not, the Company’s incremental borrowing rate shall be used.

**4.2. Lessee Accounting: Subsequent Measurement**
*   **ROU Asset:** Subsequently measured using the cost model, depreciated on a straight-line basis over the shorter of the lease term or the useful life of the underlying asset. The ROU asset is also subject to impairment testing in accordance with Ind AS 36, Impairment of Assets.
*   **Lease Liability:** Subsequently measured by increasing the carrying amount to reflect interest on the lease liability (using the effective interest method) and reducing the carrying amount to reflect lease payments made. The Lease Liability is remeasured upon certain events, such as a change in the lease term, a change in future lease payments resulting from a change in an index or a rate, or a change in assessment of an option to purchase the underlying asset.

**4.3. Practical Expedients and Exemptions (Ind AS 116 Specific Considerations)**
In line with Ind AS 116, BharatFinServ Ltd. elects to apply the following practical expedients:
*   **Short-term leases:** The Company will not recognise ROU assets and lease liabilities for leases with a lease term of 12 months or less at the commencement date and that do not contain a purchase option. Lease payments associated with these leases will be recognised as an expense on a straight-line basis over the lease term.
*   **Low-value assets:** The Company will not recognise ROU assets and lease liabilities for leases of underlying assets that are of low value (e.g., office equipment, small IT devices, and minor furniture). Lease payments for these assets will be recognised as an expense on a straight-line basis over the lease term. The assessment of "low value" will be made on an absolute basis, irrespective of the size or nature of the Company.
*   **Non-lease components:** Ind AS 116 generally requires lessees to separate lease components from non-lease components. However, for certain classes of underlying assets, and where the non-lease components are not significant, the Company *elects*, as permitted by Ind AS 116, not to separate non-lease components from lease components. Instead, the entire contract consideration will be accounted for as a lease. This election is made by class of underlying asset and will be applied consistently.
    *   *Note:* While Ind AS 116 largely aligns with IFRS 16 on these practical expedients for lessees, the Company will ensure its application is strictly in accordance with the specific guidance and interpretations issued by Indian regulatory bodies, particularly concerning the definition and threshold for "low-value" assets and the practicalities of separating non-lease components to avoid any potential conflicts or non-compliance with the Indian standard's nuances.

**4.4. Lease Term Determination and Discount Rate Application**
*   **Lease Term:** The determination of the lease term considers the non-cancellable period of the lease, periods covered by options to extend the lease if the Company is reasonably certain to exercise that option, and periods covered by options to terminate the lease if the Company is reasonably certain not to exercise that option. Management judgement is applied in assessing reasonable certainty, considering all relevant facts and circumstances.
*   **Discount Rate:** When determining the present value of lease payments, the Company will first seek to use the interest rate implicit in the lease. If this rate cannot be readily determined, the Company's incremental borrowing rate will be used. This rate will be determined at the commencement date of the lease and subsequently updated upon remeasurement events if required.

**4.5. Lessor Accounting and Presentation & Disclosure**
While BharatFinServ Ltd. primarily acts as a lessee, should it enter into agreements as a lessor, such leases will be classified as either a finance lease or an operating lease.
*   **Finance Lease:** If the lease transfers substantially all the risks and rewards incidental to ownership of an underlying asset, it is classified as a finance lease. The Company will derecognise the underlying asset and recognise a net investment in the lease.
*   **Operating Lease:** If the lease does not transfer substantially all the risks and rewards incidental to ownership, it is classified as an operating lease. The Company will continue to recognise the underlying asset and recognise lease income on a straight-line basis over the lease term, or on another systematic basis if more representative of the pattern in which benefit from the use of the underlying asset is diminished.
*   **Presentation & Disclosure:** ROU assets will be presented either within Property, Plant and Equipment or separately. Lease liabilities will be presented separately as financial liabilities, with current and non-current portions appropriately classified. Detailed disclosures as required by Ind AS 116, including a reconciliation of the lease liability, ROU asset movements, and significant judgements made, will be provided in the notes to the financial statements.

**5. Responsibilities**
*   **Finance Department:** Responsible for the implementation, monitoring, and ongoing compliance with this policy; accurate recognition, measurement, and disclosure of lease transactions; periodic review of lease contracts; and maintaining supporting documentation.
*   **Legal Department:** Responsible for reviewing lease agreements to identify lease components, terms, conditions, and options relevant to accounting treatment.
*   **Operations & IT Departments:** Responsible for providing timely and accurate information regarding new leases, modifications, and terminations.
*   **Chief Financial Officer (CFO):** Ultimate oversight and approval of this policy and ensuring its effective implementation across the Company.
*   **External Auditors:** Will review compliance with this policy and Ind AS 116 during annual audits.

**6. Review Cycle**
This policy shall be reviewed annually, or more frequently if there are significant changes in Ind AS 116, relevant regulatory pronouncements, the Company's business operations, or a material change in lease volume or complexity. Any amendments will be approved by the CFO.

**7. Effective Date**
This policy is effective from October 15, 2023, and supersedes any previous lease accounting policies.
